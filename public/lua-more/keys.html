Keys never sit in your game files

Keys live in the loader URL from your dashboard. Ship the loader, not the key — that's the whole point.

Under the hood

Why the keys can't be casually stolen

Hashed at rest

Keys are stored as one-way hashes. A database leak doesn't leak your keys.

HMAC'd HWIDs

Device fingerprints are bound by HMAC-SHA256 — we see "same device", never the raw ID.

Rate-limited checks

Loader checks are rate-limited per key and per edge IP, so brute-forcing a key isn't free.

Anti-bypass telemetry

Optional hook and tamper reports flag loaders that try to skip the check.

Instant propagation

Revocations reach every edge within ~60 seconds. No waiting for a cache to die.

Nothing to resell

Keys are tied to your project and signed load markers — a key from someone else's page won't load yours.
