Creator guides

Loading screen Wide loading bar while LuaMore authenticates and delivers your script — four presets, loader URL, mobile-wide layout.
Key system Durations, HWID binding, limits, and revocation — the full anatomy of a key.
Obfuscators Two real engines: the in-house LuaMore VM (in-browser) and the 00fuscator public API.

Protection

wYnFuscate Default Luau obfuscation, optimized for Roblox scripts. Runs on every save, on every plan, forever free.
Cached builds Protect on save, serve cached output. Repeated loads skip the obfuscation queue entirely.
Game routing Bind a script to specific PlaceIds and dispatch only at auth time for those games.

Keys

Duration Lifetime, daily, weekly, monthly, or custom windows per key or per project.
HWID Bind to a device after the first run. The fingerprint is HMAC-hashed, never stored raw.
Limits Cap executions, cap sessions, cap devices. Revoke instantly — edges pick it up in ~60s.
Discord map Tie keys to guild members so panels know who has what.

Discord

Setup Link a guild, pick a project, choose the roles that can redeem.
Roles Grant a role on successful redeem — your store workflow, minus the spreadsheet.
Loaders Deliver loader snippets via a key panel or a slash command, on demand.
Recovery HWID reset from Discord when a user rebuilds their PC (with your verification rules).

Rewards free

Ad and reward links are creator-configured — pick any provider you already use. No revenue share, no forced checkpoints.

Ad links Linkvertise, LootLabs, Work.ink, or your own. A key is issued after the visitor completes your link.
Claims Audit trail per claim: pending → verified → issued, with timestamps you can export.
Anti-replay One-time claim records and completion digests stop replayed receipts.

Adding a loading screen?

A wide loading bar that works on mobile and desktop. Add your loader URL, then tweak it if you want.
